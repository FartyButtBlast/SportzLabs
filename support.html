Common SportzLabs support questions

Where do I start with Workout Studio?

Open Workout Studio and sign in, then create a routine under Workouts. Our workout logging guide explains sets, reps, weight and completed sessions.

What should I include in a workout-history or sharing report?

Include your app version, device and browser, the approximate session date, and whether the issue concerns saved history or Strava sharing. Please do not send passwords. Check the privacy page for storage information before clearing app data.

How do I get help with AFL player statistics?

Choose AFL Performance in the form and include the team, player, statistic and date you were viewing. You can also review the AFL Performance features.

Does this support page belong to Sportzlab or SportsLab?

This is support for SportzLabs, the maker of Workout Studio and AFL Performance. We are not affiliated with organisations called Sportzlab, Sportz Lab, Sports Lab or SportsLab. See the SportzLabs brand FAQ for details.
