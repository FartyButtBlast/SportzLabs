This page covers Workout Studio and AFL Performance. The guidance below describes each app’s content and intended use. Any age rating shown in an app store is specific to that app and region; this page does not assign or replace an official store rating.

AFL Performance Overview

AFL Performance is a sports analytics application that presents current-season AFL team and player performance information, player comparison tools, charts, trend signals, and curated AFL news links.

AFL Performance Content Type

The app is designed for sports fans and people interested in AFL performance analysis. The content is informational and analytical. It does not include user-generated chat, social posting, explicit content, sexual content, graphic violence, or mature themes.

Sport And Competition Context

AFL Performance discusses professional sport, player performance, team trends, statistics, and publicly available sports news. References to competition, performance, teams, players, and match outcomes are part of normal sports analysis.

No Gambling Functionality

AFL Performance does not provide gambling, betting, wagering, bookmaker services, odds, payment facilities, or gambling transactions. It does not allow users to place bets or participate in contests with prizes.

News Links

The app may link to external AFL news articles from third-party publishers. Those publisher websites control their own content and policies. SportzLabs aims to surface reputable sports news sources and avoid unsuitable content where possible.

Parental Guidance

Parents and guardians should consider whether sports analytics, sports news, and professional player performance discussion are appropriate for the young person using the app.

Workout Studio

Workout Studio is a fitness planning and logging app for strength, cardio and mobility routines. It lets users record sets, repetitions, weights, duration and distance, review completed sessions and optionally connect Bluetooth heart-rate and temperature sensors. It does not provide gambling, betting or prize contests.

Workout Studio Parental Guidance

Workout Studio is intended for people who can choose and perform exercise safely. Parents and guardians should assess a young person’s readiness and arrange appropriate adult or qualified supervision for exercise and equipment use. A content age rating is not a recommendation that every workout, weight or training intensity is suitable for a child.

The app provides logging tools, not medical advice or a personalised assessment of exercise suitability. Sensor readings are not medical monitoring or emergency alerts. Young users should not rely on these readings to decide whether an activity is safe.

Accounts And External Sharing

Workout Studio requires an account; AFL Performance currently does not. Workout Studio’s Strava connection is optional. Strava has its own eligibility, minimum-age, privacy and sharing rules, which must be met before connecting. Parents and guardians should review any external service and its visibility settings before permitting a young person to use it. AFL news links also lead to independently controlled websites.

See our Privacy Policy for account information, analytics, sensor data and sharing, and our Terms for conditions of use for both apps.
