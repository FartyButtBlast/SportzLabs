Overview

AFL Performance is a sports analytics application that presents current-season AFL team and player performance information, player comparison tools, charts, trend signals, and curated AFL news links.

Content Type

The app is designed for sports fans and people interested in AFL performance analysis. The content is informational and analytical. It does not include user-generated chat, social posting, explicit content, sexual content, graphic violence, or mature themes.

Sport And Competition Context

AFL Performance discusses professional sport, player performance, team trends, statistics, and publicly available sports news. References to competition, performance, teams, players, and match outcomes are part of normal sports analysis.

No Gambling Functionality

AFL Performance does not provide gambling, betting, wagering, bookmaker services, odds, payment facilities, or gambling transactions. It does not allow users to place bets or participate in contests with prizes.

News Links

The app may link to external AFL news articles from third-party publishers. Those publisher websites control their own content and policies. SportzLabs aims to surface reputable sports news sources and avoid unsuitable content where possible.

Parental Guidance

Parents and guardians should consider whether sports analytics, sports news, and professional player performance discussion are appropriate for the young person using the app.

Contact

For questions about age suitability or app content, contact SportzLabs through the support page.
