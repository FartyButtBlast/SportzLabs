Exercise targets

Practical starting estimates, not pass/fail strength standards.

How to use the ranges: choose a load at the low end that lets you complete every rep with consistent technique. If you can exceed the top of the rep range by 1–2 reps for two sessions, add a small amount next time. If cycling fatigue is high, keep the load and reduce a set.
