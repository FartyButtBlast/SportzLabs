A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Simple answers.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Workout Studio runs in your browser on mobile and desktop. Open it, sign in, and start building your routine.

What can I track?

Strength exercises, cardio and stretching. Build reusable routines, check off completed sets, and review your session history.

Where are my workouts saved?

Your login is managed securely with Supabase. Workout routines and history currently stay in the browser where you created them. They do not yet sync between devices, and clearing browser data removes them.

Can I share a workout to Strava?

Workout Studio includes a Strava connection and sharing flow. Once the connection is enabled, you can review a completed workout and choose to post it. Sharing is optional and never automatic.

Where can I get help?

Visit our support page for questions, feedback or help with Workout Studio.
