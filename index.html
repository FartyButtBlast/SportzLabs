A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Simple answers.

Looking for SportzLabs after searching a similar name?

If you searched for “Sportzlab”, “Sportz Lab”, “Sports Lab” or “SportsLab” while looking for Workout Studio, you’re in the right place. Our brand is SportzLabs — with a z and a final s — and our official website is SportzLabs.com. Similar names can refer to other businesses. Look for SportzLabs.com and Workout Studio to identify our workout app.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Workout Studio runs in your browser on mobile and desktop. Open it, sign in, and start building your routine.

What can I track?

Strength exercises, cardio and stretching. Build reusable routines, check off completed sets, and review your session history.

Where are my workouts saved?

Your login is managed securely with Supabase. Workout routines and history currently stay in the browser where you created them. They do not yet sync between devices, and clearing browser data removes them.

Can I share a workout to Strava?

Workout Studio includes a Strava connection and sharing flow. Once connected, newly completed workouts upload automatically. You can turn automatic sharing off in Account settings. Heart-rate summaries are shared only if you enable them; temperature is never sent.

Where can I get help?

Visit our support page for questions, feedback or help with Workout Studio.
